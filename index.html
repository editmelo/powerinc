Who We Are

Our Mission

Programs that transform communities.

POwER provides comprehensive, holistic, and innovative programs designed to transform communities. We help empower individuals and families to reach their goals for personal and professional success while prioritizing their overall wellness and well-being.

Our Vision

Individuals and families thriving in every part of life.

We carry out this vision through affirming, empowering, and transformational learning experiences.

  • Comprehensive
  • Holistic
  • Innovative

Signature Programs

Girls STEM Institute

GSI gives scholars from elementary school through college the chance to build their understanding of math and STEM in meaningful, culturally grounded settings, with particular attention to the experiences of Black girls.

  • Elementary through college
  • Math & STEM
  • Culturally grounded

Family & Workforce Wellness Initiative

A support system that helps families balance home and work. We partner with employers to offer holistic support, so no family has to navigate life’s changes feeling overwhelmed or alone.

Built around the eight dimensions of wellness

  • Emotional
  • Physical
  • Social
  • Intellectual
  • Spiritual
  • Occupational
  • Financial
  • Environmental
